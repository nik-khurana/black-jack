📖 Hi-Lo Card Counting

Card counting tracks the ratio of high cards to low cards left in the shoe.

Card Values:

  • Low Cards (2-6): +1
  • Neutral (7-9): 0
  • High Cards (10-Ace): -1

How it works:

Keep a "Running Count" of every card dealt. Divide that by the estimated decks remaining to get the "True Count".

High True Count (+2 or more): You have the advantage! Increase your bet.
Negative True Count: The dealer has the advantage. Bet the minimum.
